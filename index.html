Play as VEER
Free-roam • No story • Pure sandbox
Tip: upload Veer's photo to put his real face on the character.
WASD + Mouse on PC • Joystick + Touch on Mobile • Gamepad supported

HOW TO PLAY

🚶 On foot (PC)

WASD move • Mouse camera • SHIFT sprint • SPACE jump
SPACE again mid-air = SUPER JUMP
F / Left-click = Melee • Q = Dash
R / Right-click = Energy Blast • C = Shield
E = Enter vehicle • P = Pause • M = Mute

🚗 Driving (PC)

W/S accelerate & brake • A/D steer
SPACE handbrake • E exit • L headlights
Gamepad: sticks + A / X / B / Y

📱 Mobile

Left stick move • drag right side for camera
Buttons: JUMP, PUNCH, DASH, BLAST, SHIELD, ENTER
In vehicle: GAS, BRK, ◀ ▶, EXIT

⭐ Wanted

Hurting people, attacking cops = stars.
Escape cops' sight to cool down.
Busted = respawn at police station.

🏁 Activities

Race track (SE), street race (downtown),
time trial, stunt ramps, parkour park (center),
60 orbs, target range (W), 5 hidden spots.

⚡ Abilities

All abilities use Energy (blue bar) and
have cooldowns. Energy refills over time
and from collectible orbs.
